Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026  |  Last updated: May 18, 2026

Note: This privacy policy is provided for informational purposes. Mo ("the App") is operated by Lexi Morgan ("we," "us," or "our"). Please read this policy carefully. By using Mo, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who We Are

Mo is an AI-powered iOS application that helps users save, organize, and act on content including recipes, events, shopping items, places, and other personal information. The App is operated by Lexi Morgan, based in the United States.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: leximorgan816@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

  • Email address and password (if you register with email)
  • Name and profile information (if you sign in with Apple or Google)
  • Authentication tokens

Content You Save

  • Screenshots, photos, and images you share to Mo
  • URLs and links you share to Mo
  • Text content extracted from your saved items via OCR (optical character recognition)
  • Notes and edits you make to saved items
  • Wishlists, favorites, and organizational data you create

AI-Generated Data

  • Titles, summaries, categories, and tags generated by AI analysis of your content
  • Extracted structured data such as recipe ingredients, event details, and product information
  • Food photo extractions and bounding box data
  • Nutrition estimates calculated by AI

Usage Data

  • App usage patterns and feature interactions
  • Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Crash reports and performance data

Optional Integrations

  • If you connect your Instagram account: your Instagram access token and any content you explicitly choose to save from Instagram
  • Calendar data accessed only when you choose to add an event (via Apple EventKit — we do not store your calendar data)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Mo app and its features
  • Process and analyze content you save using AI to generate useful structured data
  • Enable search, organization, and retrieval of your saved content
  • Send you notifications you have opted into (such as event reminders and weekly digests)
  • Authenticate your account and maintain security
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve app performance
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your saved content to train AI models without your explicit consent.

4. AI Processing and Third-Party AI Services

Mo uses AI to analyze and structure the content you save. This involves sending your content (images, extracted text, and URLs) to third-party AI providers for processing. Currently, we use:

  • Anthropic Claude API — for content analysis, categorization, recipe extraction, and other AI features. Content sent to Anthropic is processed in accordance with Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Content sent to AI providers is used solely to generate structured data for your use within Mo. We take reasonable steps to minimize the data shared and do not share data beyond what is necessary for the analysis.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Supabase, a secure backend platform. Data is stored on servers in the United States. Supabase employs industry-standard security measures including encryption at rest and in transit.

Images and files you save are stored in Supabase Storage with access controls tied to your authenticated account. Only you can access your saved content.

While we take reasonable precautions to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data and saved content within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at leximorgan816@gmail.com or by using the data deletion option within the App settings.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We share data with Supabase (data storage) and Anthropic (AI processing) solely to operate the App. These providers are contractually prohibited from using your data for their own purposes.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process.
  • Business transfer: If Mo is acquired or merged, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before this occurs.
  • With your consent: We may share information in other ways if you give us explicit consent.

  • Right to correct inaccurate data

  • Right to export your data in a portable format

EU/UK residents (GDPR/UK GDPR):

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at leximorgan816@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Mo is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

Users between 13 and 18 should use Mo only with parental consent.

10. Instagram Integration

If you choose to connect your Instagram account to Mo:

  • We request access only to permissions necessary to fetch content you explicitly choose to save
  • Your Instagram access token is stored securely and used only to retrieve content on your behalf
  • We do not post to Instagram on your behalf without your explicit action
  • You can disconnect your Instagram account at any time from Mo's settings, which will revoke our access token
  • Instagram data is subject to Meta's Privacy Policy in addition to this policy

11. Calendar Access

Mo may request access to your device calendar (via Apple EventKit) when you choose to add a saved event to your calendar. We only read and write calendar data at the moment of your explicit action. We do not store your calendar data on our servers.

12. Push Notifications

Mo may send push notifications for event reminders, weekly digests, and item analysis completion. You can control notification preferences in your device Settings at any time.

13. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the EU and UK, our legal basis for processing your personal data is:

  • Contract performance: Processing necessary to provide the Mo service you have signed up for
  • Legitimate interests: Improving app performance and security
  • Consent: For optional features such as Instagram integration and push notifications
  • Legal obligation: Where required by applicable law

14. International Data Transfers

Mo is operated from the United States. If you are located outside the United States, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States. We take appropriate safeguards to ensure your data is protected in accordance with this privacy policy and applicable law.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you within the App and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Your continued use of Mo after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

We will seek your explicit consent for any changes that materially affect how we process your personal data.
