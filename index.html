How it works

You already save everything. Mo makes it useful.

Mo is an AI memory app for real life — built for the links, screenshots, photos, social posts, products, places, and ideas you actually want to come back to.

➕

Save from anywhere

Use the iOS share sheet to send Mo links, screenshots, photos, notes, recipes, products, places, and social posts from the apps you already use.

✨

Mo understands it

AI pulls out useful details like summaries, images, captions, categories, recipe info, product details, places, source links, and actions.

🔎

Find it later

Search your saved items by keyword, category, collection, or the tiny detail you remember when you finally need it.

What Mo can do

Turn messy saves into clean, useful cards.

Mo does more than store links. It organizes the things you save so they become searchable, visual, and ready to use.

🧠

Social posts without the chaos
